What we collect

If you use the form or email us, we receive your name, email address, anything you write in your message and, if you give it, your postcode or town. We use it only to reply to you and, if you ask for it, to deal with your enquiry.

Lawful basis

Replying to an enquiry you send us is in our legitimate interests, or steps taken at your request before any agreement (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f) and 6(1)(b)).

Who processes it for us

FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers form messages to our inbox. AgentMail (agentmail.to) hosts the inbox. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log technical data such as IP addresses for security. Some of these providers process data outside the UK under their own safeguards.

Cookies and analytics

We set no cookies. We count page views with GoatCounter, which records the page, referrer and screen size but no cookies and no personal profile. It does not run if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for up to 12 months after our last contact, unless you become a customer, in which case we keep records as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it, by emailing localreply+vowtally@agentmail.to. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
